Distributed Energy Resources (DER)

Definition: Small-scale power generation/storage near point of use — opposite of centralized grid.

Examples: Rooftop solar, small wind, battery storage, micro-hydro, fuel cells, diesel gensets.

Key feature: Can operate grid-connected OR island mode (off-grid).

Key Concepts

  • Microgrid: Localized DER cluster that can disconnect and function autonomously.
  • Virtual Power Plant (VPP): Aggregation of multiple DERs managed centrally via software — behaves like a single large plant.

Benefits & India Context

  • Reduced transmission losses; resilience against grid failures; energy access in remote areas.
  • PM-KUSUM: Solar pumps for farmers | PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana: Rooftop solar subsidy.
  • CERC (Central Electricity Regulatory Commission) governs grid integration norms.
Summary: DERs = decentralized power near usage point. Microgrid = autonomous cluster; VPP = software-aggregated DERs. India promotes via PM-KUSUM & PM Surya Ghar. Regulated by CERC.

Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generator (RTG)

Definition: "Nuclear battery" converting heat from radioactive decay directly into electricity via the Seebeck (thermoelectric) effect.

Fuel: Plutonium-238 (Pu-238) — preferred because: half-life ~87.7 years (longevity); alpha emitter (safe, easily shielded).

No moving parts → extremely reliable; ideal for deep space where solar panels fail.

Nitric Oxide (NO) & Vasodilation

Vasodilation: Widening of blood vessels via smooth muscle relaxation → ↓BP, ↑blood flow.

NO production: Endothelial cells produce NO via enzyme Nitric Oxide Synthase (NOS) from L-arginine.

Signalling Pathway

NOS → NO (diffuses into smooth muscle) → activates guanylyl cyclase → ↑cGMP → muscle relaxation → vasodilation.

Medical Significance

  • Sildenafil (Viagra): Inhibits cGMP breakdown (PDE-5 inhibitor) → sustained relaxation.
  • Nitroglycerin: Releases NO → treats angina (chest pain from coronary artery spasm).

Other roles of NO

  • Immune defense (kills pathogens) | Neurotransmission | Platelet aggregation inhibition.

Nobel 1998: Furchgott, Ignarro, Murad — discovery of NO as signalling molecule.

Summary: NO = gaseous signalling molecule from L-arginine via NOS. Pathway: NO → cGMP → vasodilation. Sildenafil (inhibits cGMP breakdown), Nitroglycerin (releases NO) both exploit this. Nobel 1998.

Fig–Fig Wasp: Nature's Most Intimate Mutualism

Obligate mutualism: Fig tree (Ficus ~750 spp.) and fig wasp (Agaonidae) — cannot reproduce without each other.

Life Cycle Process

  • Female wasp enters fig (syconium = inside-out flower cluster) through ostiole, often losing wings.
  • Lays eggs in some flowers; pollinates others with carried pollen.
  • She dies inside. Male wasps hatch first → mate with females → tunnel out.
  • Females collect pollen → fly to new fig → cycle continues.

Ficin: Enzyme in fig breaks down wasp bodies — you don't eat wasp remains.

Specificity: Each fig species has dedicated wasp species (cospeciation).

India: Ficus benghalensis (Banyan), F. religiosa (Peepal), F. racemosa — all have own wasp partners.

Keystone species: Figs fruit year-round → sustain hundreds of species during lean seasons.

Summary: Fig–wasp = obligate mutualism; neither reproduces alone. Female wasp enters syconium, pollinates, lays eggs, dies. Ficin digests wasp. Each fig species has dedicated wasp. Figs = keystone species.

Poisonous vs Venomous — Key Distinction

Poisonous Butterflies

  • Müllerian Mimicry: Multiple poisonous species share warning coloration.
  • Batesian Mimicry: Non-poisonous mimics poisonous (e.g., Common Mormon mimics Crimson Rose).

Tropical Peatlands

Peat: Partially decomposed organic matter in waterlogged (anoxic) conditions — prevents decomposition → locks carbon.

Global significance: ~3% of Earth's land surface BUT ~30% of all soil carbon — twice the carbon in all forests.

Congo Basin (Cuvette Centrale)

  • Fully mapped/discovered in 2017 — largest known tropical peatland complex.
  • Covers ~145,500 km² (DRC + Republic of Congo).
  • Stores ~30 billion tonnes of carbon (= 3 years of global fossil fuel emissions).

Threats

  • Indonesia: Drainage for oil palm plantations → peat fires. 2015 fires: ~1.5–1.75 GT CO₂ (> Germany's annual emissions).
  • Peat fires burn underground for months — very hard to extinguish.
  • RAMSAR Convention: Designates important wetlands including peatlands. India: 82 Ramsar sites.
Summary: Peatlands = 3% land but 30% soil carbon. Congo Cuvette Centrale (2017) = ~30 GT C. Indonesia peat fires 2015 = 1.5-1.75 GT CO₂. Threats: drainage + fire. RAMSAR protects.

Coriolis Force

Definition: Apparent force due to Earth's rotation; deflects moving objects.

Rule: Right in Northern Hemisphere; Left in Southern Hemisphere (Ferrel's Law).

Named after: Gaspard-Gustave de Coriolis (1835).

Magnitude: Zero at equator → maximum at poles. Proportional to wind speed and sin(latitude).

Effects

ApplicationEffect
Trade WindsNE trades in NH (deflected right)
CyclonesAnticlockwise NH; Clockwise SH
Ocean GyresClockwise NH; Anticlockwise SH
Jet StreamsWest-to-east flow (Coriolis + pressure)
River banksRight bank more eroded in NH (Baer's Law)

Geostrophic Wind: Coriolis balances pressure gradient → wind flows parallel to isobars.

No cyclones within 5° equator — Coriolis too weak to initiate spin.

Summary: Coriolis: deflects right in NH, left in SH. Cyclones: anticlockwise NH, clockwise SH. Zero at equator → no tropical cyclones within 5°. Baer's Law: NH rivers erode right bank more.
